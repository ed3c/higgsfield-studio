## 道具與時間連續性

### `founder_coat_A`

- 連續性：S03 and S17 worn by founder; S21 already hanging on chair when shot starts; founder wears plain charcoal shirt sleeve; S23 not worn; S24 same design raised by crowd. Distinct from archive coat B.
- 特徵鎖：plain charcoal work coat, unique subtle cuff-repair stitching near LEFT hem; no signature leather look
- 鏡頭：S03, S17, S21, S24

### `archive_coat_B`

- 連續性：S10 archive copy from earlier period; different short cut and cuff wear; must never be confused with A.
- 特徵鎖：shorter straight charcoal cotton coat, no left hem repair; shelf glasses generic
- 鏡頭：S10

### `filament_bird_v1`

- 連續性：Same original irregular wire joins and filament placement in S04 and S24; no resemblance to Mockingjay or other emblem.
- 特徵鎖：asymmetric abstract bird, one warm filament, three very small wire joints, not a logo silhouette
- 鏡頭：S04, S24

### `optical_membranes`

- 連續性：S03 three-layer open, S17 more layered, S23 nearly dormant; NEVER suspended rectangular tank or original artwork replica.
- 特徵鎖：curved translucent polymer glass blades with condensation and physically real caustics
- 鏡頭：S03, S17, S23

### `paper_house_v1`

- 連續性：Tiny paper house in S11 foreshadows S22 paper-city craft method and warm window; not a physically transported prop.
- 特徵鎖：uneven folded roof and small nonrectangular single window, plain office paper
- 鏡頭：S11, S22

### `handover_key`

- 連續性：One key in S21 only, original simple design.
- 特徵鎖：small undecorated brass key, no corporate insignia
- 鏡頭：S21

### `warm_reflection`

- 連續性：S02 wet steel -> S03 membrane -> S04 suitcase -> S05 water -> S06 light -> S17 optical -> S23 floor -> S24 rain pavement. Cause is real local light, not magical light travelling locations.
- 特徵鎖：thin broken warm line, never identical scene replication
- 鏡頭：S02, S03, S04, S05, S06, S17, S23, S24

### `untuned_piano`

- 連續性：S13 coastal hall aged piano, S23 distant piano dolly is echo/transported object, no literal inter-country transport claim.
- 特徵鎖：weathered upright with worn lower wooden panel and one incomplete-return key
- 鏡頭：S13, S23

### `opera_coat`

- 連續性：S14 costume/archive motif remains indirect; S23 on rolling rack held by anonymous worker.
- 特徵鎖：antique dark opera coat, muted design without movie reference
- 鏡頭：S14, S23

## 重要的防錯約束

- S10 的 B 外套是檔案副本；S03/S17/S21/S24 的 A 外套才是主線原物。
- S21 的外套 A 已在 S17 到 S21 的畫面時間空隙由主角脫下，S21 開頭起始終掛在椅上；遞鑰匙的手只能穿無特徵深色襯衫袖口。
- S11 與 S22 是紙屋製作方法相同，不代表它是從工廠被物理搬到另一個國家。
- S13 的技術工藝不是對日本整體經濟能力的事實判決。
- S19 比賽版更換為虛構城市，不呈現真正新聞、政治人物或所謂會面現場。
- 比賽版 S03/S17/S23 不得使用參賽者可辨識的真人實拍 plate；使用完全新生成虛構角色。
